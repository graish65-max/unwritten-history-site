What this tool does

One show, one account, published on schedule.

Unwritten History Publisher generates narrated "what if" short-form video content — history, science, space, and society reimagined — and publishes it to the TikTok account @unwrittenhistoryyt, the official TikTok presence for the Unwritten History video series.

Every video is produced and reviewed before it goes out: a script, a narration track, and a set of visuals, assembled into a short episode and posted to the one account this tool is authorized for.
