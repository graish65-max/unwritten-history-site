Unwritten History Publisher publishes narrated short-form video content to the @unwrittenhistoryyt TikTok account, the official TikTok presence for the Unwritten History video series.

1. Scope

The application connects to one TikTok account, which it is authorized to publish to on behalf of the Unwritten History series. It does not accept sign-ups from other users and does not publish to any account other than the one it is authorized for.

2. Use of TikTok APIs

The application uses TikTok's Login Kit to authenticate the connected account, and the Content Posting API to publish video content that has already been produced to that same account.

3. Content

All video content published through this application is produced and reviewed — narration, visuals, and editing — before it is uploaded. The operator is solely responsible for the content published to the connected account and for compliance with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. No warranty

This application is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind.

5. Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to the operator via the @unwrittenhistoryyt account.
